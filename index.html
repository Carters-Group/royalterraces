The vision

A terrace street that feels like it was always here

Nine homes side by side, each with its own front door to the street, a garden at the gate and a balcony above. The facades are painted in soft heritage colours and trimmed with wrought-iron lace and timber friezes. Each parapet carries a plaque, and you can choose the name on yours.

Classic terrace character outside, light and easy living inside.

Upstairs, the kitchen, dining and living flow onto a covered balcony. Downstairs there's a second lounge, so there's room to work from home, host guests or give everyone their own space. Behind the homes is a shared garden, and every home has its own car space off the street.

Energy efficiency

Comfortable all year, with less heating and cooling

The nine homes at 6 Royal Street have a 7.9 star average energy rating under NatHERS, the national energy rating scheme for homes. Every home is rated above the 7-star standard, from 7.1 up to 8.4 for the courtyard home, and seven of the nine are rated 7.8 stars or higher. The more stars a home has, the less heating and cooling it needs to stay comfortable, which means lower power bills through a Toowoomba winter and summer.

Seven stars is the current standard for a new home in Queensland. Most of these homes go well beyond it.

Location

Everything you need, on foot

Royal Street sits on the north edge of Toowoomba's city centre. The grocer and two cafés, The Baker's Duck and Ortem, are two minutes away, The Farm park and Grand Central five, the primary school seven; the CBD and a run of good cafés are all within ten.
