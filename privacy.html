Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by TN Macartney Projects Pty Ltd (ABN 68 657 387 258), the developer and seller of the terraces at 6 Royal Street, Toowoomba City QLD 4350. In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean TN Macartney Projects Pty Ltd.

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

When you request an information pack or contact us, we collect:

  • your first and last name, email address and phone number
  • the home you are interested in, and whether you are buying to live in, investing or downsizing
  • whether you would like to hear from us about future developments
  • anything else you tell us by email, phone or in person, such as questions, inspection times or a proposed name for your terrace's plaque

You can browse the website without giving us any personal information. You don't have to give us your details, but without them we can't send you a pack or reply to you.

How we use it

  • to send you the information pack you asked for and answer your questions
  • to contact you about 6 Royal Street: availability, prices, inspections, progress and the sales process
  • if you agree, to tell you about our future developments
  • to prepare contracts and selections paperwork if you decide to buy

We don't sell your personal information or give it to anyone for their own marketing.

Marketing and unsubscribing

We will only send you news about future developments if you tick the box on our form or otherwise tell us you'd like it. Every marketing email will include a way to unsubscribe, and you can ask us to stop at any time using the contact details below.

How we store it

Enquiries sent through this website are delivered to us by email. We record them in an enquiries register kept on a password-protected computer at our office in Queensland, Australia. Only people working on the sale of 6 Royal Street can access it. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access.

Who we share it with

We share your information only when we need to, and only with:

  • a licensed real estate agent appointed to sell the terraces
  • our solicitor or conveyancer, and the builder, if you buy a home
  • companies that provide our website hosting and email services
  • anyone we are required or authorised to share it with by law

Information that may go overseas

Our website host and email providers may store or process information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. This website also loads its fonts from Google, which means your browser's IP address is sent to Google's servers when a page loads.

Cookies and website data

This website doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our website host keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, browser type and the pages visited, to run and secure the site.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while 6 Royal Street is being sold and built. If you agreed to hear about future developments, we keep them until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. When we no longer need your information, we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

Seeing or correcting your information

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. We will reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Complaints

If you are concerned about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first. If you're not satisfied with our response within 30 days, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
